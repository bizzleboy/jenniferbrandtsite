To understand oneself and to be understood by another is what leads to healing. A therapist is both your teacher and your student. As you teach me about yourself, we learn together who you are, and what you need. I bring an experienced and sensitive perspective that helps you discover, use, and enjoy your strengths as you face the challenges in your life. I aim to connect with clarity, wisdom, and compassion.

Life's changes are an opportunity for personal growth, often requiring us to expand our understanding of who we are and what our purpose is. The choices you make during these times of transition give you the power to create the life you want to live.

Transitions frequently brought to treatment include:

  • Marriage
  • Loss of a job
  • Divorce
  • An affair
  • Birth
  • Death
  • A New Job
  • Graduating
  • Relocating
  • Illness

The process of therapy brings to light what is and isn't working well in one's life, and why. These are the stepping stones on the path to uncovering your truth and your strengths:

  • Awareness
  • Compassion
  • Authenticity
  • Purpose
  • Intimacy
  • Humor
  • Inspiration
  • Clarity
  • Healing
